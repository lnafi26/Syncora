Before the first cut

The soundtrack shapes the edit before the timeline does.

Finding the right song and deciding what moments deserve a visual change can quietly consume a huge amount of editing time. Synchora gives that planning stage a place of its own.

It does not edit for you. It helps narrow the possibilities, surface useful musical structure, and give you a clearer starting point while your creative judgment stays in control.

Crown Jewel 02

Turn a song into an editing map.

Pulsar helps you see a song through the eyes of an editor.

A song can contain hundreds of beats, but only some moments deserve a major visual change. Pulsar organizes the useful ones into a Signal so you can see where the edit might cut, transition, accelerate, breathe, or shift direction without treating the suggestion as a command.

Use it for Cuts, reveals, transitions, speed changes, effects, and text.
Pulsar provides Prioritized opportunities, not creative commands.
You keep Full control over the final edit.
